# Late deliveries are a major fixable driver of bad reviews

**Recommendation:** Prioritise delivery performance in Alagoas, Maranhão and Piauí. Late orders are approximately 5.9 times as likely to receive a 1–2 star review as on-time orders.

## Key findings

1. **Strong revenue growth:** Monthly revenue grew by 672.6% between January 2017 and August 2018, peaking at R$1.15 million in November 2017, coinciding with Black Friday.
2. **Low customer retention:** Only 3.0% of customers placed more than one order, indicating a heavy reliance on new customer acquisition.
3. **Delivery performance and customer satisfaction:** Late deliveries received an average review score of 2.57/5, compared with 4.29/5 for on-time deliveries — a difference of 1.72 stars (p < 0.001). Furthermore, 54.1% of late deliveries received a 1–2 star review, compared with 9.2% of on-time deliveries.

## Recommendations

1. **Prioritise regional delivery improvements.** Investigate logistics bottlenecks and carrier performance in Alagoas (23.87% late), Maranhão (19.55%) and Piauí (15.97%).
2. **Improve delivery estimates.** Set realistic promised delivery dates using historical delivery times and regional logistics performance.
3. **Encourage second purchases.** Launch a targeted retention campaign for the "New" RFM customer segment through personalised offers and post-purchase communication.

## Limits of this analysis

The dataset covers Brazilian e-commerce transactions from 2016 to 2018 and may not reflect current market conditions. Review scores may also be influenced by product quality, seller performance and customer service. Statistical significance establishes an association, not causation. Regional comparisons exclude states with fewer than 100 eligible orders.
